Filipino Fried Chicken

Description

This is the fried chicken you'll find at almost every Filipino gathering — chicken marinated in calamansi (or lemon), garlic, and fish sauce, then fried until the skin turns deep golden and crackly. It's simpler than American-style fried chicken since there's no wet batter, just a dry rub and a hot pan, but the flavor from the marinade carries all the way through the meat. Best eaten hot with steamed rice and a side of banana ketchup or spiced vinegar for dipping.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces (leg quarters, wings, or drumsticks)
  • 1 whole lemon or 4-5 calamansi, juiced
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce (patis)
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • Cooking oil, for frying
  • Banana ketchup, for serving (optional)

Steps

  1. Squeeze the lemon or calamansi juice over the chicken pieces, making sure every piece is coated. Let sit for about 30 minutes.
  2. In a small bowl, mix the fish sauce, garlic, salt, and pepper. Rub this all over the chicken, working it under the skin where you can.
  3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or overnight for deeper flavor.
  4. Heat about an inch of oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
  5. Fry the chicken in batches, about 12-15 minutes per side, until the skin is deep golden brown and the meat is cooked through.
  6. Drain on a plate lined with paper towels to remove excess oil.
  7. Serve hot with rice and banana ketchup or spiced vinegar on the side.
