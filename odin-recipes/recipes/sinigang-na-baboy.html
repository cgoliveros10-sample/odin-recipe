Sinigang na Baboy

Description

Sinigang is a sour soup that a lot of people consider just as much a national dish as adobo. This version is made with pork ribs simmered until tender in a broth soured with tamarind, then loaded with vegetables like kangkong, radish, eggplant, and string beans. It's the kind of dish that's perfect on a rainy day, served with rice and a small dish of fish sauce and chili on the side for dipping the meat.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork ribs or pork belly, cut into chunks
  • 8 cups water
  • 2 tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 1 pack tamarind soup base (sinigang mix), or fresh tamarind pulp
  • 1 radish, sliced
  • 1 eggplant, sliced
  • 1 bunch string beans (sitaw), cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 bunch kangkong (water spinach)
  • 2-3 finger chilies
  • Fish sauce (patis) and salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Place the pork in a large pot with the water and bring to a boil, skimming off any scum that rises to the top.
  2. Add the tomatoes and onion, then lower the heat and simmer for about 1 hour, or until the pork is tender.
  3. Stir in the tamarind soup base (or strained fresh tamarind juice) until dissolved.
  4. Add the radish and let it cook for a few minutes until slightly softened.
  5. Add the eggplant, string beans, and chilies. Simmer for another 3-5 minutes.
  6. Season with fish sauce and salt to taste.
  7. Add the kangkong last and cook for about a minute, just until wilted.
  8. Serve hot with steamed rice and extra fish sauce on the side.
